Nickel privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026. Nickel is made by SUPERRDUPERR.

What Nickel does on your device

Nickel reads the product names and prices on the Amazon, Target and Walmart search pages you open, and works out the best price per unit. Your settings are stored in your browser through Chrome's sync storage.

What Nickel sends to us

Unless you turn off Share searches to improve Nickel in the Nickel popup, Nickel sends one record per search to our review service. The record contains:

  • the words you searched for, and how Nickel read them;
  • the product names, prices and unit prices shown on the results page, and what Nickel highlighted;
  • the site (Amazon, Target or Walmart), the page address without its query string, and the Nickel version.

It does not contain your name, email, account, cookies, delivery address, order history or anything you type into a site other than the search.

Why

To find searches where Nickel picked the wrong product, and fix them. An AI model (Anthropic's Claude) reviews the records once a day. They are not used for advertising and are not sold.

How long we keep it

Search records are kept for 30 days, then the raw text is deleted. We keep only the review result (for example, "wrong match") and the count of searches.

Your choice

Open the Nickel popup and untick Share searches to improve Nickel. Nothing is sent after that. If you feedback-report a search, the report you copy is yours to send or not.
